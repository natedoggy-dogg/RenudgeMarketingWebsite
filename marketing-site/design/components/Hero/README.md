# Hero

The top of every page: an `eyebrow` naming who it's for, a `display-xl` headline, a `lede`, a primary and a secondary button, and one `small` reassurance line.

- One hero per page, one `display-xl` per page. Headline ≤ 8 words; lede ≤ 2 lines at desktop width.
- The eyebrow names the audience, not the category ("For mortgage brokers and bookkeepers", not "AI document collection").
- On desktop the hero's visual (usually `ReplyToData` or `PhoneFrame`) sits to the right at 5/7; on mobile it follows the buttons.
- The reassurance line must be true on the day it ships (no "free" line until the trial terms are set).
- Consumer provides: eyebrow, headline, lede, two CTAs with URLs, reassurance line, optional visual.
