# Steps

A numbered sequence for "How it works": serif `numeral` in `nudge`, a `display-s` title and a short muted paragraph, each step opened by a hairline.

- Three to five steps. Titles are what happens, in the operator's terms; paragraphs ≤ 2 lines.
- The numerals are the only place `nudge` colours type on the page.
- On the Product page, each step can expand into a full section with its visual; on the homepage they stay compact.
- Consumer provides: an ordered list of title and body.
