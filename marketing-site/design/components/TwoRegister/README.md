# TwoRegister

Two columns split by a hairline that speak to the two personas separately: the practitioner (relief from drudgery) on the left, the buyer (revenue and risk) on the right.

- Never blend the registers in one column. Left is about time and tedium; right is about money, visibility and audit.
- Eyebrows name the person by what they do, not their title, so it works across verticals.
- Use once per page at most. Testimonials follow the same split when they exist: one practitioner quote, one owner quote.
- Consumer provides: two eyebrows, two headlines, two paragraphs.
